Our Story

Our Story

Coffee&Beans was founded in 2019 by Thandiwe mokoena a former corporate IT worker who followed her passion for coffee and trained as a barista in Cape Town.

What started in Braamfontein grew into a second cafe in melville, locally sourced coffee while taking a moment to slow down.
