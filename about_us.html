WHO WE ARE

Good Coffee. Meaningful Moments.

Coffee&Beans is a welcoming coffee brand founded in 2019 by Thandiwe Mokoena. Our story began with a simple idea: to create a place where people could enjoy quality coffee and feel at home.

Based in Johannesburg, we bring together carefully selected coffee, friendly service and local favourites. Whether you are preparing for a busy day, meeting a friend or taking a well-deserved break, we want every visit to feel special.

We believe that a good cup of coffee can create meaningful moments, bring people together and make an ordinary day feel a little brighter.
