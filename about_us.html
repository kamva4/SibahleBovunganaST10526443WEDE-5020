Our Story

Coffee&Beans began with a simple idea: great coffee should give people a moment to slow down.

Meet our Founder

Thandiwe Mokoena

Thandiwe Mokoena founded Coffee&Beans in 2019 after leaving her corporate IT career and training as a barista in Cape Town.

Our Journey

2019 - The Beginning

Our first Coffee&Beans cafe opened in Braamfontein.

2021 - Melville

Two years later,we opened our second cafe in Melville.

The Future

Our vision is to grow into a network of neighbourhood cafes across South Africa.

Our Mission

To serve high-quality, locally sourced coffee to busy professional womaen in Johannesburg while giving them a space to slow down between work and meetings.

Our Vission

To build a welcome coffee brand that bridges generations,from students and young creatives to working professional,while eventually expanding across South Africa.
